## Dart and Flutter packages

All packages in `pubspec.lock` are under permissive licenses (MIT, BSD-3-Clause
or Apache-2.0), except `dbus` (MPL-2.0, pulled in by `file_picker` for Linux
only, used unmodified, so nothing to publish). None is GPL/LGPL or
non-commercial. Flutter collects their `LICENSE` files, and this app's own
`LICENSE`, into the license page automatically. It does not see native
libraries that packages download at build time, which is why PDFium's
licenses are registered by hand.
