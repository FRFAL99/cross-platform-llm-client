The phone-cluster encryption (ggml-rpc/privatelm-crypto.h) follows two
public-domain works:

- TweetNaCl (X25519), by Daniel J. Bernstein, Bernard van Gastel,
  Wesley Janssen, Tanja Lange, Peter Schwabe and Sjaak Smetsers.
  Public domain. https://tweetnacl.cr.yp.to

- poly1305-donna, 32-bit version, by Andrew Moon.
  Public domain. https://github.com/floodyberry/poly1305-donna

ChaCha20-Poly1305 follows RFC 8439.
